About the shop

A small shop with a big soft spot for animals.

Rafi A Pets sits on Kagmari Road in the heart of Tangail. It is the kind of shop where you can ask a question without feeling rushed — what to feed a new budgie, why a fish tank went cloudy, or which cage is the right size for a pair of lovebirds.

The shelves carry what local pet owners genuinely need, and the doors stay open around the clock — because pets do not always have emergencies during shop hours.

  • Care before the sale. We would rather send you home with the right advice than the wrong cage.
  • Stock that stays fresh. Feed is stored properly and checked before it reaches the shelf.
  • Open when you need us. Call at any hour for stock, prices or a quick bit of guidance.
